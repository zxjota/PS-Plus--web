Mobile App Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 8, 2026

At Ktomaqut, your privacy is a priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our mobile application, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services and a seamless experience. The types of data we handle include:

  • Information you provide directly: such as your name, email address, or profile details when you create an account or contact support.
  • Usage data: app interactions, feature usage, crash reports, and performance data (e.g., app version, device model).
  • Device & network info: operating system, unique device identifiers, and approximate location (only if you grant permission).
  • Storage access: with your consent, we may access files you choose to open or share within the app.
📱 No sensitive personal data — we do not collect health, financial, or biometric information. We never sell your personal data.

2. How We Use Information

Ktomaqut uses collected data to operate, maintain, and improve our services. Specifically, we use data to:

  • Provide core functionality (e.g., syncing your preferences, remembering settings).
  • Respond to support requests and customer service inquiries.
  • Analyze app performance, fix bugs, and enhance user experience.
  • Send important service notifications (e.g., updates or security alerts) if you've opted in.

We do not use your data for targeted advertising or share it with third parties for marketing.

3. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share limited data only in these circumstances:

  1. Service providers: trusted partners (e.g., cloud storage, analytics) who help us run the app, under strict confidentiality.
  2. Legal compliance: if required by law, regulation, or enforceable governmental request.
  3. Business transfers: in case of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred with notice.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards — including encryption in transit (TLS) and secure storage — to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g., disable location or storage permissions).
  • Opt out of non-essential communications.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

You can exercise these rights by contacting us using the email below. We respond within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Ktomaqut is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in certain regions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy in the app or via email. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest revision.

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding your privacy, feel free to reach out. We're happy to help.
